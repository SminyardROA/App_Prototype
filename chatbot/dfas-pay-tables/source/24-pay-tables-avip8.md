#### **Monthly Navy Officer Aviation Incentive Pay Rates**

**(Effective April 1, 2018)**

| --- | --- |
| **Years of Aviation Service** | **Monthly Rate** |

| **Over 6** | 650 |
| **Over 14** | 840 |
